How and why we use text messaging — and how to opt in or out at any time.

About the Program

Post-Service Satisfaction Surveys

Montie Wayne Sheet Metal & Heating uses SMS (text messaging) to send brief post-service satisfaction surveys to customers who have voluntarily opted in. These messages help us make sure every job meets our standards and give you a direct, easy way to share feedback.

We send no more than 3 messages per completed job: an initial check-in, one follow-up if we haven't heard back, and — if needed — a short escalation for any unresolved concerns.

We will never use your number for promotional or marketing messages, and we will never share or sell your information to third parties.

How to Opt In

Optional Consent on Your Work Order

Consent to receive SMS messages is entirely voluntary and is separate from scheduling, payment, or any other service agreement with Montie Wayne Sheet Metal. You will still receive full service whether or not you opt in.

At the time of service, customers are asked to initial the following section on the paper work order if they wish to participate:

Work Order — Optional SMS Consent

Optional: Would you like to receive a brief post-service satisfaction survey via SMS? Initial here to opt in: ___

Customer initials (optional)
Date

By initialing the box above, you agree to receive up to 3 automated SMS messages per completed job from Montie Wayne Sheet Metal at the mobile number you provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase or service.

Program Details

What to Expect

  • Message Frequency Up to 3 messages per completed job. Message frequency may vary.
  • Message & Data Rates Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.
  • Who Messages You Messages are sent from Montie Wayne Sheet Metal & Heating, (805) 968-2215 area, via a dedicated business number registered with our SMS provider.
  • Purpose Post-service satisfaction surveys only. We do not send promotional offers or marketing messages via SMS.
  • Shared / Sold Your mobile number is never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing purposes.

Opt-Out & Help

Stop Messages at Any Time

You may opt out at any time by replying to any message with one of the following keywords:

STOP CANCEL QUIT UNSUBSCRIBE END

After texting STOP, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. You may re-enroll at any time by initialing a new work order or contacting us directly.
