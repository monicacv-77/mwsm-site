Privacy Policy

Montie Wayne Sheet Metal & Heating

Last updated: October 26, 2026  ·  Contractor Lic. #660236

Section 1

Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information in the course of providing our services:

  • Name
  • Phone number (including mobile number, when provided)
  • Email address
  • Property address or service location
  • Service history and job details

We collect this information when you contact us for a quote, schedule service, or sign a work order.

Section 2

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Schedule and perform requested services
  • Communicate with you about your job or appointment
  • Send post-service satisfaction surveys via SMS (only if you have voluntarily opted in on your work order)
  • Respond to questions or complaints
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

Section 3

SMS Communications

If you have opted in to receive SMS messages by initialing the consent section of your paper work order, we may send you up to 3 automated text messages per completed job. These messages are limited to post-service satisfaction surveys only.

  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message
  • We do not send promotional or marketing SMS messages
  • Message and data rates may apply

We do not use your mobile number for any purpose other than the post-service survey program described above. Full opt-in program details are available at mwsm-site.vercel.app/sms-opt-in.

Section 4

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With our SMS service provider (Twilio) solely to deliver text messages you have consented to receive
  • As required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • To protect the rights or safety of our company or customers

Section 5

Data Storage & Retention

Customer information is stored in our business management software and associated records. We retain customer records for as long as necessary to provide services and meet legal obligations, typically no longer than 7 years.

SMS opt-in records — including the date and method of consent — are retained for a minimum of 4 years to comply with TCPA recordkeeping requirements.

Section 6

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt out of SMS communications by replying STOP to any message
